The short version

I run Kova Media: paid media, creative, and the tracking that connects the two, so an owner can look at one number and know what it bought.

I came up through sales before I came up through marketing. Phones, objections, follow-up, close. I learned what makes somebody say yes long before I learned what makes an ad cheap, and most media buyers never learn it in that order.

The creative is the campaign. The reporting is the relationship.

Get those two right and everything else is arithmetic. Outside the ad accounts I build my own tools, write my own scripts and shoot my own content. I'd rather understand a thing end to end than rent someone else's version of it.
